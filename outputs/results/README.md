# Final Research Results

This folder contains the final analytical results, research findings, statistical summaries, and tables generated as part of the project **A Multimodal Analytics Framework for Long-Term Entertainment Trend Discovery in Indian Cinema (2000–2025)**.

The results are based on the ICMB-130 benchmark dataset comprising 130 selected Indian films spanning 2000–2025.

### Research Scope

The analyses examine:

- Long-term commercial performance of selected Indian films
- Nominal versus CPI-adjusted India Gross trends
- Differences in commercial performance across film rankings
- Dialogue-derived cinematic themes and their evolution over time
- YouTube audience sentiment patterns
- Exploratory associations between cinematic themes, audience sentiment, and commercial performance

## Interpretation

The results are intended for exploratory research and analytical interpretation. Statistical associations should not be interpreted as causal relationships. Commercial performance, dialogue themes, and audience sentiment represent different analytical dimensions of the selected film benchmark.

## Dataset

The results are derived from the ICMB-130 benchmark dataset containing 130 selected films from 2000–2025.

The benchmark focuses on selected high-grossing films and does not represent the complete Indian film industry.
